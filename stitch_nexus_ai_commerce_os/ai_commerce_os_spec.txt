# AI Commerce Operating System (AI Commerce OS) - Product Specification

## Overview
A comprehensive, AI-driven commerce platform designed to automate the entire lifecycle of online business—from product discovery to fulfillment and analytics.

## Core Pillars
1. **AI Engine ("The Brain"):** Central intelligence handling LLM gateway, recommendations, trend prediction, and business advice.
2. **Product Intelligence:** Real-time scanning for trends, competitors, and demand.
3. **Multi-Hub Ecosystem:** Dedicated hubs for Marketplace, Suppliers, Marketing, Sales, CRM, Automation, Inventory, and Finance.
4. **Developer & Enterprise:** White-labeling, API gateways, and enterprise-grade multi-tenant architecture.

## Key Workflow Integration
- **Detection:** AI identifies viral products.
- **Analysis:** Margin and risk calculation.
- **Creation:** AI Content Factory generates copy, images, and videos.
- **Distribution:** Auto-posting to marketplaces (Shopee, Tokopedia, TikTok, etc.).
- **Engagement:** AI-driven CRM and auto-replies.
- **Fulfillment:** Automated order routing to suppliers/warehouses.
- **Optimization:** Executive dashboards with real-time KPI and forecasting.

## Architecture
- **Distributed Databases:** Domain-specific DBs (auth, commerce, inventory, etc.) for microservice scalability.
- **AI Gateway:** Dynamic model routing (OpenAI, Gemini, Local LLM) based on task and cost.
